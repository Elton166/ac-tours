About AC Tours

Meet Ashley, your local Cape Town tour guide.

Welcome

My name is Ashley, and I am a passionate local tour guide based in beautiful Cape Town, South Africa.

I have been proudly involved in the tourism industry since 2005, bringing over 20 years of experience, knowledge and passion to every journey. As a registered Local Guide for the past two years, I take great pride in sharing the beauty, history, culture and hidden gems of Cape Town with visitors from around the world.

My goal is to offer more than just a sightseeing tour. I want every guest to experience the true spirit of Cape Town through its incredible landscapes, rich heritage, diverse cultures, fascinating history and warm hospitality.

Whether exploring the Cape Peninsula, discovering the Cape Winelands, experiencing local township culture, enjoying a city tour or heading out on a safari adventure, I always aim to make every trip comfortable, memorable and special.

I believe that every guest deserves personal attention, friendly service and an unforgettable experience.

All tours are done privately, so each trip feels personal, relaxed and tailored to your pace, interests and travel style.

With Ashley as your guide, you don’t just visit Cape Town – you experience it!

I look forward to welcoming you to our beautiful Mother City.

AshleyYour Local Cape Town Tour Guide
